## 2. Purpose of the Study

This research project aims to create and analyse a dataset that supports the study of how piano skills develop over time. The project focuses on **knowledge tracing**, a computational approach used to model learning progress and improve intelligent educational systems.

By participating, you will contribute to research that may advance both the theory and practical application of intelligent music learning technologies.

---

## 3. What Participation Involves

If you choose to participate, you will use our piano learning platform to complete a series of piano practice exercises, either:

* **online**, using your own electronic piano with MIDI capability; or
* **in person**, using electronic keyboard equipment provided by the research team.

During your practice sessions, the system will automatically collect basic interaction and performance data, including:

* notes played in **MIDI format**;
* timing accuracy;
* duration of practice;
* task completion sequence; and
* related interaction logs generated through the platform.

No audio will be recorded. MIDI data captures performance events only and does **not** record background sound or voice.

Participation does not require any additional action beyond normal platform use.

---

## 4. Participant Eligibility

You may participate if you:

* are willing to use the piano learning platform for piano practice;
* have access to an **electronic piano with MIDI recording capability**, or are able to attend an in-person session using provided equipment; and
* provide informed consent before data collection begins.

Participants may include ANU students and external volunteers.

We anticipate recruiting approximately **10 to 100 participants**, subject to volunteer availability.

---

## 5. Duration and Location

Participation may occur remotely through the designated learning platform or in person using equipment provided by the research team.

Each individual piano exercise is expected to take approximately **1 to 15 minutes**. Participants may engage with the exercises at times convenient to them, subject to platform availability and study arrangements.

---

## 6. Voluntary Participation and Withdrawal

Participation in this study is entirely voluntary.

You may:

* decline to participate;
* decline the use of your data for research purposes; or
* withdraw from the study at any time **before your data has been anonymised**,

without providing a reason and without penalty.

Withdrawal requests may be submitted through the piano learning platform. If you withdraw:

* no further data will be collected from you; and
* previously collected data may be deleted upon request, provided anonymisation has not yet occurred.

The current withdrawal cut-off date is **1 April 2026**.

---

## 7. Use of Data

The collected data will be used to:

* analyse patterns in piano learning and skill development;
* improve music knowledge tracing models;
* support research in intelligent music education systems; and
* generate findings for academic dissemination.

Research outputs may include:

* peer-reviewed journal publications;
* conference presentations; and
* the investigator’s Master thesis.

Once study findings are published, a link to the published results may be made available through the interactive piano learning platform so that participants and the broader public can access the outcomes.

---

## 8. Risks and Inconveniences

### Privacy and Data Security

The primary risk associated with this study is the potential risk to privacy. This risk is minimised through the following measures:

* no audio recording;
* anonymisation of research data before analysis;
* restricted access to identifiable information; and
* secure storage of all research materials.

During data processing, identifying information will be replaced with a unique participant code.

A separate linkage table connecting participant identities to these codes will be stored securely and used only for:

* participant recruitment;
* participant management; and
* withdrawal processing.

Access to this linkage table is restricted to authorised members of the research team under strict conditions. If you withdraw from the study, your corresponding identifiable information in the linkage table will be permanently deleted where applicable.

### Minor Inconvenience

A minor inconvenience is the time required to review the Participant Information Sheet and consent materials before beginning the exercises. These documents will be available on the platform at account creation so that you may review them at your own pace.

## 11. Confidentiality

Access to participant data will be strictly limited to authorised members of the research team.

To protect participant privacy:

* all data will be anonymised prior to analysis;
* personal identifiers will be removed and replaced with randomly assigned codes; and

Research findings will be reported in aggregate or de-identified form. Where necessary, details will be further generalised to reduce the risk of re-identification.

## 13. Data Storage and Retention

### Where Data Will Be Stored

Data will be collected through the piano learning system during participants’ engagement with practice activities.

Processed research data will be securely stored in **Supabase** cloud storage under the management of the research team.

Access to stored data will be restricted to:

* **Nanxuan Xie**; and
* **Dr Bernardo Pereira Nunes**.

### Retention Period

All research data and associated consent records will be retained for **at least five years from the date of publication**.

### After the Retention Period

After the required retention period, aggregated results and de-identified data may be securely archived within the Supabase environment for future research use strictly related to this study.

Any archived data will remain subject to restricted access. If consent forms are to be disposed of, they will be securely destroyed by the supervisor or in accordance with university procedures.
